# Findings

> Canonical scientific knowledge: what the project is prepared to rely on.
> Includes both positive and negative results. Agent-entered findings arrive
> by PR. Every entry states its claim precisely, its scope—the assumptions or
> conditions under which it holds—and links its provenance: an investigation,
> notes, or a citation key. Do not rewrite entries: mark them superseded or 
> refuted and link the replacement. Technical and methodological choices
> belong in `DECISIONS.md`.
